Decision modelling and scenario analysis Interactive model, illustrative inputs

Tipping Point

Where should the next rupee of budget go?

What the workflow does
Turns each input into a range, runs 5,000 scenarios, finds the best split inside the model, shows where each route stops paying back, ranks the assumptions by how much they matter, and writes a one-page memo.
What it needs
For each of three routes: sales at half the budget, margin and how fast returns fade, each as a low, typical and high value. Also the current plan.
What it produces
A recommended split, the trade-off against the current plan, a stopping point for each route, the key uncertainty and a memo.
Automated and manual
The calculations are automated. The numbers are yours to supply or replace, and a person judges whether the model is fair to the business.
Method and assumptions
Each route is an independent curve of diminishing returns, with inputs drawn from triangular ranges using a fixed seed, so a rerun gives the same answer. The best split is the best of 66 splits in steps of 10%. There is no overlap between routes, no brand effect and no fixed cost.
Limits and status
The result is the best split inside the model and not a forecast. It has not been compared with real outcomes. The hotel case is the default, and pharma, telecom and food cases are available. Inputs entered stay in your browser.

Shiftwatch

What changed in the market this week, and does it matter for our decisions?

What the workflow does
Reads a fixed list of public sources, logs each event with a date, a source and a status, names what is not disclosed, ties events to the decisions you track and drafts a memo.
What it needs
A list of sources, and the decisions you want events tied to.
What it produces
A dated event log, a list of gaps and a memo that links each change to a decision.
Automated and manual
The recorded run shows what a workflow produced. A person picks the sources and approves each flag.
Inputs and method
A hotel-sector run on public sources, recorded and checked on 29 October 2026. Each event carries one of six statuses, and a gap is logged as a gap.
Limits and status
It is a recording and fetches nothing when you open it. The data was supplied and has not been re-checked against its sources here. A workflow version exists and has been tested end to end only against mock services. Accuracy is not measured.

Condition Watch

How strong is the evidence for each question about a condition, and what is still missing?

What the workflow does
From the condition you name, writes a set of questions and a source list. As findings are logged, it rates each question by written rules and builds an evidence board and a memo.
What it needs
A condition, and the findings, which a person enters.
What it produces
An evidence board, a source list and a memo.
Automated and manual
The rating, the board and the memo update automatically. Finding and entering the evidence is manual, because it reads no sources.
Inputs and method
Ratings follow written rules: strong, mixed, inference only, or nothing disclosed yet. The rules were run against boundary cases, and changing an entry was checked to update the board and the memo.
Limits and status
The search links open other sites, and what comes back has not been reviewed. Its example entries are invented. It is a prototype and not a verified medical review.

Guidance Tracker

Did the company deliver what it said it would?

What the workflow does
Scores each stated target against the reported result by written rules, builds a record by topic and shows what a full-year range implies for the rest of the year.
What it needs
Targets, dates and reported results, taken from real documents. A ledger can be imported and exported as a CSV file.
What it produces
A score for each target, a record by topic and a memo.
Automated and manual
Scoring is automatic. Entering targets and results is manual, and a person can overrule any score.
Inputs and method
Targets count as met, partly met, missed, pending or overdue under written tolerances. The rules were run against boundary values, and typing a result was checked to change its score, the record and the memo.
Limits and status
It checks no entry against a document, and its example figures are invented. It tracks stated targets, which can be financial guidance or commitments such as launches. It is not investment advice.
