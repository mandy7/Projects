Four examples, four kinds of analyst work

The demo on the home page runs four decisions across three industries. These four examples go further into one decision each. Open one, change the inputs and watch the output change. None of them asks for an email address.

Two of the demo decisions, testing a price change and ranking options, run only in the demo for now.

Hotels

Shiftwatch: the weekly market scan, as a record

Replaces
An analyst reads the trade press every week and notes what changed.
You get
A dated log of events, a list of what is not disclosed, and a memo that links each change to a decision.
A person still
Picks the sources and approves each flag.
Status
Recorded research run on public sources, checked on 29 October 2026. It does not fetch anything when you open it. A workflow version exists and has been tested end to end only against mock services.
Open Shiftwatch

Telecom

Tipping Point: the budget split, tested

Replaces
A spreadsheet that holds one best guess per input.
You get
5,000 what-if cases across three sales channels, the spend at which each channel stops paying back under your assumptions, and the assumption the answer depends on most.
A person still
Supplies the real numbers, judges whether the model is fair to the business, and makes the call.
Status
Interactive model with illustrative inputs, which you can replace with your own. Each channel is modelled as an independent curve, so the result is the best split inside the model and not a forecast.
Open Tipping Point

Pharma

Condition Watch: how strong is the evidence for each question

Replaces
An analyst searches trial registries, regulators and company releases, and rebuilds the list whenever the scope changes.
You get
Questions and a source list written from the condition you name, an evidence board that rates each question and changes as you log findings, and a memo.
A person still
Reads the sources and enters what they find.
Status
Interactive prototype with manual evidence entry. It reads no sources, and its example entries are invented.
Open Condition Watch

Consumer goods

Guidance Tracker: what was said against what was reported

Replaces
Someone compares last year’s targets with this year’s results, when they remember to.
You get
A score for each target as soon as its result is entered, a record by topic that shows how reliable the company has been, and what a full-year range implies for the rest of the year.
A person still
Enters targets and results from real documents, and can overrule any score.
Status
Interactive prototype with manual data entry. It checks no entry against a document, and its example figures are invented. It tracks stated targets, which can be financial guidance or commitments such as launches. It is not investment advice.
Open Guidance Tracker
