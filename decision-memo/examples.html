Tipping Point

Where should the next rupee of budget go?

Turns each input into a range, runs 5,000 scenarios, and finds the best split inside the model, where each route stops paying back and which assumption matters most.

You supply the numbers. A person judges whether the model is fair to the business.

Inputs and output
In: a low, typical and high value for each route’s sales, margin and how fast returns fade, plus your current split. Out: a recommended split, the trade-off against your plan, a stopping point for each route, the key uncertainty and a one-page memo.
Method and limits
Each route is an independent curve of diminishing returns, drawn from triangular ranges with a fixed seed, so a rerun gives the same answer. The result is the best split inside the model and not a forecast, and it has not been compared with real outcomes. The hotel case is the default, with pharma, telecom and food cases available.

Shiftwatch

What changed in the market this week, and does it matter for our decisions?

Reads a fixed list of public sources, logs each event with a date, a source and a status, names what is not disclosed, and ties events to the decisions you track.

A person picks the sources and approves each flag.

Inputs and output
In: a list of sources and the decisions you want events tied to. Out: a dated event log, a list of gaps, and a memo that links each change to a decision.
Method and limits
A hotel-sector run on public sources, recorded and checked on 29 October 2026. It fetches nothing when you open it, and the data has not been re-checked against its sources here. A workflow version exists and has been tested end to end only against mock services. Accuracy is not measured.

Condition Watch

How strong is the evidence for each question about a condition, and what is still missing?

Writes a set of questions and a source list from the condition you name, then rates each question by written rules as findings are logged.

A person finds the evidence and enters it, because the tool reads no sources.

Inputs and output
In: a condition, and findings entered by a person. Out: an evidence board, a source list and a memo.
Method and limits
Ratings are strong, mixed, inference only, or nothing disclosed yet, under written rules that were run against boundary cases. The search links open other sites, and what comes back has not been reviewed. Example entries are invented. It is not a verified medical review.

Tracking commitments Prototype, manual entry

Guidance Tracker

Did the company deliver what it said it would?

Scores each stated target against the reported result, builds a record by topic, and shows what a full-year range implies for the rest of the year.

A person enters targets and results from real documents, and can overrule any score.

Inputs and output
In: targets, dates and reported results, which can also be imported as a CSV file. Out: a score for each target, a record by topic and a memo.
Method and limits
Targets count as met, partly met, missed, pending or overdue under written tolerances, tested at their boundaries. It checks no entry against a document, and its example figures are invented. It tracks stated targets, which can be financial guidance or commitments such as launches. It is not investment advice.
