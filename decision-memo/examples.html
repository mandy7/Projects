Flagship example · Hotels

Tipping Point: where should the next rupee go?

A hypothetical hotel chain has ₹100 crore to place across direct bookings, online travel agents and corporate sales. The model tests 5,000 versions of the future and returns a split, the trade-off, the assumption that matters most and what to check next.

What it replaces
A spreadsheet that holds one best guess per input, and the meeting where each person argues for a different guess.
What you get
A recommended split, the spend at which each route stops paying back under your assumptions, how often the split beats the current plan, and the one assumption the answer depends on most.
What still needs a person
Supplying the real numbers, judging whether the model is fair to the business, and making the call.
Current status
Interactive model with illustrative inputs, which you can replace with your own. Each route is modelled as an independent curve, so the result is the best split inside the model and not a forecast. Also set up for pharma and telecom cases.

Supporting examples

Hotels

Shiftwatch: the weekly market scan, as a record

What it replaces
An analyst reads the trade press every week and notes what changed.
What you get
A dated log of events, a list of what is not disclosed, and a memo that links each change to a decision.
What still needs a person
Picks the sources and approves each flag.
Current status
Recorded research run on public sources, checked on 29 October 2026. It does not fetch anything when you open it. A workflow version exists and has been tested end to end only against mock services.

Pharma

Condition Watch: how strong is the evidence for each question

What it replaces
Searching trial registries, regulators and company releases by hand, and rebuilding the list whenever the scope changes.
What you get
Questions and a source list written from the condition you name, an evidence board that rates each question as you log findings, and a memo.
What still needs a person
Reads the sources and enters what they find.
Current status
Interactive prototype with manual evidence entry. It reads no sources, and its example entries are invented.
Open Condition Watch

Consumer goods

Guidance Tracker: what was said against what was reported

What it replaces
Comparing last year’s targets with this year’s results, when someone remembers to.
What you get
A score for each target as soon as its result is entered, a record by topic showing how reliable the company has been, and what a full-year range implies for the rest of the year.
What still needs a person
Enters targets and results from real documents, and can overrule any score.
Current status
Interactive prototype with manual data entry. It checks no entry against a document, and its example figures are invented. It tracks stated targets, which can be financial guidance or commitments such as launches. It is not investment advice.
