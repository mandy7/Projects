Working together

Most engagements start with a conversation and a one-page memo. After that, we pick one decision and run it for a short pilot.

The exploratory audit

A free first conversation

We spend about 45 minutes on the decisions your team keeps reopening. Within a few days I send you a one-page memo that covers which of them could run as a standing watch or model, what evidence each would need, what it would take to set up, and which I would leave alone.

I need three things from you: the decisions, who reads the output today, and what the team does by hand now.

A short pilot

One real decision, no charge, for the first few teams

The pilot has a start date, an end date and a written list of what we measure: hours spent before and after, items the workflow missed, and items it got wrong. A weekly watch needs about four weeks to show change over time. A one-off model needs about two.

I would rather give you numbers from your own decision than claim savings in advance.

Setup, running and changes

An engagement has three parts. Setup covers the decisions, the sources, the rating rules and the memo format. Running covers the scans or models and the delivered memos. Changes cover anything you ask me to adjust later.

I price after the audit, because the cost depends on the number of sources, how messy they are, how many decisions are tracked, how much review you want, and whether your own data is involved.

Where it runs

There are three ways to run a workflow.

  • I run it for you. You send a simple data template or approve items by email, and you receive the memo as a PDF or an email. Nothing is installed on your side.
  • It runs inside your company’s own AI tools, after your security review. This takes longer because your IT team has to approve it.
  • Your team runs it on its own systems from files I hand over.

I would start with the first option. The examples on this site show the method. A live engagement is built and tested for your sources, and I confirm the timeline only after scoping.

Your data

I begin with public sources, which needs nothing from you.

If your own numbers are involved, we agree in writing what I receive, where it is processed and when it is deleted, before anything is shared. I ask for the least data that will do the job, and for channel or sales figures that usually means monthly totals, with no personal data.

The four examples on this site run in your browser and send nothing anywhere. If you enter your own figures into them, they stay on your device.

Good to know

Whether it is live monitoring

The examples on this site are not. In an engagement the sources are read on the schedule we agree.

Whether a person reads the memo

Yes, a named reviewer on your side approves it before anything is released, and I check it before it reaches them.

Whether it can replace an analyst

It covers the record-keeping and the first pass. The reading of what matters, and the decision, stay with your team.
