Common questions

What is the free audit?

A focused 45-minute conversation about one decision your team keeps coming back to. Within a few days I send you a one-page memo on what a practical workflow could do, what evidence it would need, what would still need human judgement and what it would take to test the idea.

What does it cost?

Work is priced as a setup fee plus a subscription for running it. I price after the free audit, because the cost depends on the sources, the data quality, the decisions tracked, the review you want and any integration. I start with the smallest useful test and build nothing large before the value is clear.

How long does it take to set up?

I confirm a timeline after scoping. It depends on how many sources are involved and how clean your data is.

Can you use our internal data?

The analyst can combine public sources with your internal data, and every item shows which kind it is. If your own numbers are involved, we agree in writing what I receive, where it is processed and when it is deleted, before anything is shared. I ask for the least data that will do the job, which for channel or sales figures usually means monthly totals with no personal data.

Can it be adapted to our situation?

The decisions, the sources, the assumptions and the memo format are set with you, and more decisions can be added over time. The demo on the home page shows four decisions, and the same approach carries to others.

Is the demo using real data?

The demo and the four examples run in your browser on illustrative data, and nothing is fetched or sent anywhere. In an engagement the sources are read on a schedule we agree.

How do I know it is not making things up?

Every item carries a date and a source, and a gap is shown as a gap. Scoring and rating rules are written out, a named reviewer approves each result, and you can ask for a back-test: give me a past period where you know what happened, and we count what the workflow missed and what it got wrong.

What error rate should I expect?

It has not been measured. A pilot on your own decision would measure what the workflow misses and what it gets wrong.

Does it learn from its mistakes?

The workflows are designed so that a reviewer’s corrections are logged and used to adjust the rules. The demo and the examples on this site do not learn. They run fixed rules on fixed examples.

Does it replace our analyst?

It covers the first pass: collecting, dating, scoring, testing and drafting. The reading of what matters, and the decision, stay with your team.

Where does it run?

I can run it for you, so you send a data template or approve items by email and receive the memo as a PDF or an email. It can also run inside your company’s own AI tools after your security review, or on your team’s systems from files I hand over. I would start with the first option.

Do you run pilots?

For selected problems, a short pilot can show whether the approach works with real inputs. We agree the scope, the dates and the measures in advance, including what the workflow misses, what it gets wrong and how much human review it needs. A pilot is offered selectively after the first conversation, and it is not a commitment to a free build.
