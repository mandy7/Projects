Book a free audit

A focused 45-minute conversation about one decision your team keeps coming back to.

Within a few days, I send you a one-page memo. It outlines what a practical workflow could do, what evidence it would need, what would still need human judgement and what it would take to test the idea.

To make the conversation useful, I ask what decision needs to be made, who uses the output today and how the work is done now.

If there is a fit, we can test it

For selected problems, a short pilot can show whether the approach works with real inputs. We agree the scope, the dates and the measures in advance, including what the workflow misses, what it gets wrong and how much human review it needs.

A pilot is offered selectively after the first conversation, and it is not a commitment to a free build.

Pricing

I scope and price work after I understand the problem. The cost depends on the sources, the data quality, the decisions tracked, the review you want and any integration.

I start with the smallest useful test, and I build nothing large before the value is clear.

Where it runs

  • I run it for you. You send a simple data template or approve items by email, and you receive the memo as a PDF or an email. Nothing is installed on your side.
  • It runs inside your company’s own AI tools, after your security review. This takes longer because your IT team has to approve it.
  • Your team runs it on its own systems, from files I hand over.

Your data

I begin with public sources, which needs nothing from you.

If your own numbers are involved, we agree in writing what I receive, where it is processed and when it is deleted, before anything is shared. I ask for the least data that will do the job, which for channel or sales figures usually means monthly totals with no personal data.

The examples on this site do not send what you type to any server.
