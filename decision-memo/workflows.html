Four workflows

Each one is a working example on a hypothetical company or invented figures. The method carries over to a real case once the sources, the decisions and the data are yours.

Hotels

Shiftwatch

The job

Keep track of what is changing in a market and which standing decisions it touches. The example watches AI agents booking hotel rooms, for a hypothetical mid-size hotel chain in India.

By hand today

Someone reads trade press and company announcements each week, notes what happened and where they saw it, and tries to remember what was never disclosed. The record usually lives in one head or a scattered document.

What the workflow does

It reads a fixed list of public sources and logs each event with its date, its source link and a status: disclosed, opinion, inference, company stated or scheduled. It names what is not disclosed, ties each change to a standing decision with an evidence rating, and drafts a one-page memo.

What a person still does

Chooses the sources and the decisions to watch, approves or holds each flag before anything is released, and decides what to do about it.

Status

A replay of one recorded run on public sources, checked on 29 October 2026. The page fetches nothing when you open it. A workflow version exists and has been tested end to end only against mock services, not against live sites or a live model service.

Telecom

Tipping Point

The job

Decide how to split a budget across routes to market and see at what spend each route stops paying back. The example is a hypothetical mid-size mobile operator placing ₹100 crore across retail and dealers, digital, and partner bundles.

By hand today

A spreadsheet with one best-guess number per input, a few what-if cases run one at a time, and an answer chosen without knowing which assumption it depends on.

What the workflow does

It treats each assumption as a range and draws 5,000 scenarios from the ranges. It finds the spend at which each route stops paying back, ranks the assumptions by how far each one moves the best split, and ends with a one-page memo. The memo names the assumption to check first and the evidence that would settle it.

The arithmetic is plain code with no AI model in it, and a rerun gives the same result.

What a person still does

Supplies the real inputs, judges whether the model’s simplifications are fair, accepts or holds each finding, and makes the budget decision.

Status

Live in your browser on invented inputs. You can change the routes, the unit and all nine ranges, or import a CSV, and nothing leaves your browser. The model treats each route as an independent curve and counts only the first year of subscriber revenue.

Pharma

Condition Watch

The job

Keep a watch on a clinical condition and the decisions that depend on it. The example starts with narcolepsy and shows what changes when you add insomnia.

By hand today

An analyst searches trial registries, regulators, company releases, conference abstracts and payer calendars, then writes up what changed. When the scope widens, the search list and the questions are rebuilt by hand.

What the workflow does

It rewrites the standing decisions and the source plan from the conditions you name. It keeps a dated ledger with a status on every entry, rates the evidence behind each decision by written rules, shows which kinds of source have no entries yet, and writes the memo.

What a person still does

Reads the sources, enters or approves what goes into the ledger, and decides on each decision. On this page you add the entries yourself.

Status

Live in your browser. It reads no source. The example entries are invented and describe a fictional compound from a fictional company. A managed version would read the sources for you.

Consumer goods

Said and Done

The job

Keep a dated record of what a company said it would do and what happened. The example is a hypothetical consumer-goods company with invented figures.

By hand today

Guidance sits in earnings notes and slide decks. Someone compares it with later results when they remember to, and the comparison is rarely kept.

What the workflow does

It holds each commitment with its date, terms and due date. It scores each as met, partly, missed, pending or overdue by rules fixed in advance, and builds a profile by topic with the number of outcomes beside every rate. It also works out what a full-year range implies for the rest of the year.

What a person still does

Enters the commitments from real documents, overrules any score with a reason, and reads the profile with its small counts in mind.

Status

Live in your browser on invented figures. The tool checks no entry against a source. It is a record-keeping aid and not investment advice.
