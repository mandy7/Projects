A Different Way to Do Intelligence Work

Businesses have three broad ways to handle recurring research and intelligence work. Each has its place, depending on the question, available resources and level of expertise required.

Option 1

General-purpose AI tools

Useful for research, exploration and individual tasks. But recurring work still requires someone to provide context, organise sources, check findings and assemble the output each time.

Option 2

Analyst teams and research firms

Bring experience, depth and judgement to complex questions. However, maintaining that level of involvement for routine monitoring and recurring analysis can be expensive or difficult to justify.

Option 3

Configured intelligence workflows

Second Analyst is designed for work that needs to happen repeatedly. The scope, sources, rules and outputs are defined in advance. A configured workflow handles the repeatable steps, while a person reviews exceptions and applies judgement where needed.

Why not use a general-purpose AI tool?

General-purpose AI tools are useful for individual research and analytical tasks. But recurring work still requires someone to provide context, organise sources, check findings and assemble the output each time.

Second Analyst configures and operates a defined process around your recurring requirement. The sources, scope, checks and outputs are agreed in advance, so the work can be repeated without rebuilding the process each time.

The distinction is not access to AI. It is the operating process, evidence discipline and delivery built around it.

The distinction
The aim is not to replace analysts or automate every decision. It is to make recurring intelligence work more structured, consistent and manageable, without requiring a dedicated team for every job.

Second Analyst is a managed intelligence service built around configured, AI-assisted workflows. Customers bring recurring business questions. The scope, sources, checks, outputs and frequency are agreed in advance. Configured workflows handle the repeatable work, while human review, interpretation and advisory support are available where required.

Experience behind the work

Second Analyst is founded by Mandhir Dua, an independent strategy and competitive-intelligence practitioner.

His experience includes working on global competitive strategy and intelligence at Expedia Group, alongside independent advisory work with smaller businesses. This experience spans understanding competitive markets, assessing strategic options, interpreting commercial evidence and helping decision-makers identify the implications that matter.

Second Analyst brings that experience into a more structured and repeatable way of handling intelligence work. The objective is not to remove human judgement, but to make the groundwork more consistent and manageable.

Who is this designed for?

Second Analyst is particularly relevant to growing and mid-sized businesses that need regular market, competitor or commercial intelligence but do not have dedicated capacity for every requirement.

It can also support established strategy and commercial teams that need additional capacity for specific recurring jobs.

Initial industry examples: travel, hospitality and online marketplaces.
