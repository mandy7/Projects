Privacy notice

What we collect, why, and what you can ask us to do with it. Written in plain language. This is not legal advice.

Who we are

Second Analyst is run by Mandhir Dua, based in Gurugram, India. For privacy questions, write to mandhir@secondanalyst.in.

What we collect

When you request a diagnostic: your name, email, company, what you want to monitor or decide, and anything you write. When you ask for a memo or the sample pack: your email. Nothing else. This site sets no cookies and runs no analytics. Fonts and video are hosted by us. What you type into Try it out stays in your browser unless you ask us to email you the memo.

Why, and for how long

We use it to reply to your request, send what you asked for and, if you go ahead, set up the work. We do not send newsletters or share your details for marketing. Your enquiry is held in Google Forms and Sheets and in our email. We keep it for 12 months unless you become a client, and delete it sooner if you ask.

Your rights

You can ask to see what we hold about you, correct it, or have it deleted, and you can object to how we use it. Write to the address above. If you are not happy with our answer, you can complain to your data protection authority, such as the ICO in the UK.

If you become a client

We agree in writing where your data is processed, who can see it, how long we keep it and whether any outside AI service reads it. Most of our work uses public sources about companies. Where a source names a person, such as an executive appointment, we use only what is published and needed for the question.
