These are capability areas, not a promise that every engagement includes every service. The scope of each engagement depends on the business question, the available evidence and data, and the deliverable you need.

Two ways to engage

Recurring

A monthly programme

For questions that keep coming back. Briefs on a schedule agreed with you, reviewed by an analyst, month to month.

One-off

A strategic engagement

For a decision in front of you now. A scenario model, a go-to-market assessment, a market-entry analysis or a decision memo, scoped separately.
