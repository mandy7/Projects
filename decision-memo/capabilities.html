Customers

Question

Who are our customers, what do they need, and how should we serve each group?

Recurring or one-off
Typical work
  • Customer groups built from your own data
  • Needs and willingness to pay by group
  • Review and survey themes

Distribution and supply

Question

Which channels, partners and suppliers should we use, and on what terms?

Recurring or one-off
Typical work
  • Marketing and sales spend split across channels

  • Supplier prices, minimums and lead times
  • Go-to-market plans and business cases with scenarios
You get

Options compared on cost, reach and risk, with ranges and the assumption each answer depends on.
