Pricing questions

How is the price built?

A shared service fee each year, plus a yearly fee for each job. Setup is one-time: a shared onboarding fee once, and a small configuration fee for each job. Frequency and the amount of material change the yearly fees. Analyst work is billed separately.

Is setup charged every month?

No. Setup is charged once. It is charged again only for a new setup or a material reconfiguration, such as changing a job’s sources or rules, at half the job’s setup fee.

How firm is this?

It is an indicative model built on assumptions I have not yet tested against real delivery cost. The proposal that follows your brief replaces it, and depends on source coverage, how often it runs, data volume and the review you want.

Can I start with one job?

Yes, and that is the usual way in. Choose one job, see whether it earns its place, and add others later.

How long does setup take?

I confirm a timeline after scoping. It depends on how many sources are involved and how clean your data is.

What error rate should I expect?

It has not been measured. A pilot on a real job would measure what the workflow misses, what it gets wrong, and how much human review it needs.

Do you run pilots?

For selected jobs, a short pilot can show whether the approach works with real inputs. We agree the scope, the dates and the measures in advance. A pilot is offered selectively after the first conversation, and it is not a commitment to a free build.
