Pricing questions

How is the price built?

A flat one-time setup fee of $1,000, and a yearly fee for each job you add. Each job is priced at about 30% of what the same work would cost to do by hand. With every job selected, the recurring cost is at most $18,000 a year. Analyst work is billed separately.

Why a range?

The final figure depends on scope, complexity and how often a job runs. The top of the range is the most it would cost at the standard scope, and the proposal after your brief gives a firm figure.

Is setup charged again?

Not for adding jobs later. A new setup or a material reconfiguration, such as changing a job’s sources or rules, is quoted separately.

How firm is this?

It is an indicative model built on assumptions I have not yet tested against real delivery cost. The proposal that follows your brief replaces it.

Can I start with one job?

Yes, and that is the usual way in. Choose one job, see whether it earns its place, and add others later.

What error rate should I expect?

It has not been measured. A pilot on a real job would measure what the workflow misses, what it gets wrong, and how much human review it needs.
