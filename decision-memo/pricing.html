How the work is checked

What analyst review covers

Every delivered brief is reviewed by a designated analyst. The review covers source relevance, supporting evidence, assumptions, conflicting information and the interpretation presented. Where evidence is incomplete or conflicting, this is made visible rather than presented as a definitive finding.

You remain responsible for the business decision.

What counts as one recurring question?

One business question with one purpose, one output and one schedule. For example: "What did our three main competitors change in their pricing this week?"

A competitor is one named company. A source is one website, feed or document set we monitor. A data connection is a file, sheet or feed of your own that we read.

What happens when a source breaks?

The workflow flags it, we investigate and address it, and your brief shows the gap clearly. A gap is never hidden. Conflicting or incomplete evidence is disclosed in the same way.
