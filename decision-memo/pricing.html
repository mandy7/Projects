What does a recurring workflow fee cover?

Each recurring fee covers the agreed workflow: running the defined job, checking the specified sources, organising evidence, applying agreed rules and delivering the required output. One-time setup defines and configures the job. Additional human review or advisory support is scoped separately.

Pricing questions

How is the price built?
Each recurring fee is shown as a range that reflects the scope, frequency and complexity of the workflow. Setup is a single fee. All figures are provisional and are confirmed once the scope is agreed.
What is included in a workflow?
Each engagement defines the business question, sources, frequency, checks and output in advance. The recurring fee covers execution and delivery within that agreed scope. Additional sources, more frequent runs, new outputs or specialised analysis may require a change in scope. Any change in cost will be discussed before the additional work begins.
Can I start with one job?
Yes. One job is enough to begin, and further jobs can be added later within a revised scope.
Can the scope change?
Yes. Additional sources, more frequent runs, new outputs or specialised analysis may require a change in scope. Any change in cost is discussed before the additional work begins.
Is setup charged again?
Setup is a single fee, however many jobs you choose at the start. A further setup charge applies only if a later job needs genuinely new configuration, and that is discussed before any work begins.
What does optional analyst involvement cover?
Human review, interpretation and advisory support beyond the agreed workflow, for example a deeper read of a result or a conversation about what it means for a decision. It is scoped and billed separately, and it is not included in the calculator.
Can you support a requirement that is not shown here?
The examples on this site illustrate the types of intelligence work we can undertake. Each engagement is scoped around the business question, available sources, frequency and required output. New or specialised requirements can be assessed separately.
