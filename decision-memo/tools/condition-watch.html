Condition Watch

Name a condition and the watch sets itself up: the standing decisions, the source plan and the rating rules. Keep a dated ledger as you read, and the memo builds from it.

Live in your browser. This page does not fetch or read any source. The ledger entries in the example are invented, about a fictional compound from a fictional company. In an engagement the desk reads the sources and you review what it finds. Nothing you type leaves your browser.

    Activity

    Change the condition and the decisions below rewrite themselves. Try adding a second condition, such as insomnia, and watch the questions and the source plan change. Edit any decision directly if you prefer your own wording.
