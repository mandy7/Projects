Condition Watch

Shows how strong the evidence is behind each question your team has about a medical condition.

  1. Name a condition, and the questions your team must answer and the sources to check write themselves.
  2. Log what you find, one row per finding, with its source and a status such as disclosed or not disclosed.
  3. Read the evidence board on the right. Each question shows how strong its evidence is, and it changes as you log.

Interactive prototype with manual evidence entry. It reads no source, so you find the evidence and enter it. The entries in the example are invented, about a fictional compound from a fictional company. Nothing you type leaves your browser.
