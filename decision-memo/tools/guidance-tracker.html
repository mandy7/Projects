Examples / Guidance Tracker

Guidance Tracker

Compare stated targets with reported results, using explicit scoring rules and a reviewable record. It tracks numeric ranges and dated milestones, and ends in a one-page memo.

Live in your browser. The example is a hypothetical consumer-goods company with invented figures. Replace it with your own ledger under the button. Nothing you enter leaves your browser. It is a record-keeping aid and not investment advice.

Use your own ledger (optional)
    Activity

    What happens when you press run

    The workflow reads the ledger, applies fixed scoring rules to each commitment, optionally waits for a reviewer to overrule any score, builds a profile by topic with the count shown beside every rate, works out what the current full-year guidance implies for the rest of the year, and writes the memo. The rules are shown below before any score appears.
