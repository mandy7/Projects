Guidance Tracker

Scores what a company said it would deliver against what it reported, and shows how reliable its record has been.

  1. List the targets a company set, such as a growth range or a launch date.
  2. Enter each result as it is reported. The score appears beside the target at once.
  3. Read the record by topic in the bar below. It updates as you type, and the memo at the bottom follows.

Interactive prototype with manual data entry. It checks no entry against a company document. The example is a hypothetical consumer-goods company with invented figures. Nothing you type leaves your browser. It is not investment advice.

How a target is scored
Met
The result is inside the target range, or on the right side of a limit such as "at least 5".
Partly
A little outside, by no more than a quarter of the range or 3 percent of a single limit. For a date, up to 90 days late.
Missed
Further outside than that, or more than 90 days late.
Pending, overdue
With no result, a target is pending until the day after its due date, then overdue. Overdue is not counted as missed.

Record by topic

In each bar, dark means met, grey means partly, rust means missed and pale means no result yet. A percentage appears only when a topic has at least four results.

Targets and results

Each row is one target. Type a result in the Result column, or change a target, and the score updates. You can overrule a score in the last columns, and the memo lists every override.
