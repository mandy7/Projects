A different way to do intelligence work

Businesses have three broad ways to handle recurring research and intelligence work. Each has its place, depending on the question, available resources and level of expertise required.

Option 1

General-purpose AI tools

Useful for research, exploration and individual tasks. But recurring work still requires someone to provide context, organise sources, check findings and assemble the output each time.

Option 2

Analyst teams and research firms

Bring experience, depth and judgement to complex questions. However, maintaining that level of involvement for routine monitoring and recurring analysis can be expensive or difficult to justify.

Option 3

A managed intelligence service

Second Analyst is designed for work that needs to happen repeatedly. The scope, sources, rules and outputs are defined in advance. Configured workflows handle the repeatable steps, and a designated analyst reviews every delivered brief.

Why not use a general-purpose AI tool?

General-purpose AI tools are useful for individual research and analytical tasks. But recurring work still requires someone to provide context, organise sources, check findings and assemble the output each time.

Second Analyst configures and operates a defined process around your recurring requirement. The sources, scope, checks and outputs are agreed in advance, so the work can be repeated without rebuilding the process each time.
