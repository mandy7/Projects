From question to reviewed brief

You tell us the job once. We run it for you every month, and a designated analyst reviews every brief before it reaches you. There is no login and no software to set up.

What comes from you, and what comes from us

You send a short brief. We do the rest.

Example: a monthly competitor pricing report.

You provide
We provide

Processing and analysis, with comparable products matched and the assumptions listed

Is this software I log into? No.

Second Analyst is a managed service. We configure and run the workflow, and an analyst reviews every delivered brief. You do not build or operate anything.

How the arrangement runs

1

Agree the scope

The question, the competitors and the sources we may use.

2

Configure and test

We set up the workflow and check it on past data where it exists.

3

It runs on schedule

Pages are collected and analysed on the dates agreed.

4

An analyst reviews

They check the evidence and investigate anything flagged.

5

You decide

You receive the brief and make the call.

What we can and cannot see

  • We use pages anyone can open, such as pricing pages, plan pages, announcements and filings.
  • Prices behind a login, or given by quote, are marked as not available.
  • Prices that vary by region or customer are reported as observed, with the conditions noted.
  • Where products are not directly comparable, we say so and show our assumption.

Where your data lives

Before you share anything, we agree in writing where your data is processed, who can see it, how long we keep it and whether any outside AI service reads it. You can ask for deletion at any time. Read the data questions

Questions you may be asking

Data and privacy

Is my data safe?
Anything you type into the try it out page stays in your browser. For a real engagement, we agree in writing where your data is processed, who can see it and when it is deleted, before you share anything. We do not hold a security certification, and we will not imply that we do.
Does my data go to an AI company?
The try it out page calls no AI model. In a real engagement, if a language model reads text, we tell you which one, what is sent to it and where it is processed, and that goes into the written agreement. Each quote it extracts is checked against the source text.
Can I have my data deleted?
Yes. We agree a retention period for each engagement, and you can ask for deletion at any time. We confirm in writing when it is done, and we ask only for the data the work needs.
What does this website store about me?
There are no analytics scripts, and the host may keep standard server logs. A message sent through the form is held by the form service and in our email, and is used only to reply.

Working with us

What do I need to provide?
Your recurring question, the competitors or topics you care about, and any documents or figures you want included. If you have historical data, it can be used to test the workflow. We set up the sources and rules with you.
Will it plug into the tools we already use?
Not today. No integration with your internal systems has been built. Where a data connection is needed, such as a sheet or a file feed, we scope it separately and say so before you commit.
How quickly can we start?
After the free diagnostic, a pilot takes about three weeks and includes two reviewed briefs.

Limits

What if it gets something wrong?
Every finding carries a date and a source, so you can check it. Gaps and conflicting evidence are shown, not hidden, and a designated analyst reviews every delivered brief. Where you provide historical data, we test the workflow against known periods where suitable. That shows how it handled the past. It is not a forecast. Accuracy has not been measured yet, and we will not quote a figure until a pilot has measured it.
Do I still need my own analyst?
Often, yes. The workflow and the analyst review cover the repeating work: gathering, dating, checking and drafting. Reading what matters for your business, and the decision, stay with your team.
How is this different from using ChatGPT myself?
A general AI tool starts from scratch each time. Here the job is set up once, with the same sources, checks and format on every run, and a designated analyst reviews each delivered brief. Use a general tool for one-off questions, and this for the questions that keep coming back.
