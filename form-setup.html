Form setup helper

This page runs only on your computer. Open it by double-clicking the file. It sends nothing anywhere. Do not upload it to your site.

Step 1. Build the Google Form

  1. Go to forms.google.com and start a blank form. Title it "Contact".
  2. Add four questions, in this order: a Paragraph question "What would you like to talk about?" (required), then three Short answer questions: "Your business or industry" (leave this one optional, because the site does not require it and Google would silently reject an empty required answer), "Email or LinkedIn profile" (required), and "Source" (optional).
  3. In Settings, make sure "Collect email addresses" is off and the form is open to anyone with the link.
  4. In the Responses tab, link the form to a Google Sheet, and use the three-dot menu to turn on email notifications for new responses.

Step 2. Get the pre-filled link

  1. In the form editor, open the three-dot menu at the top right and choose "Get pre-filled link".
  2. Type exactly AAA in the first question, BBB in the second, CCC in the third and DDD in the fourth.
  3. Click "Get link", then "Copy link", and paste it below.

Step 3. Paste the result

Open assets/config.js in your site's repository, replace everything in it with the text below, and commit the change.
